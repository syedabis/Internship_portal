## Evaluation & Scoring Structure

All projects share a standardized, transparent 5-pillar evaluation framework:

1. **Strategic Depth & Problem-Solving Framework (25%)**: Rigor of research, hypothesis framing, and business logic.
2. **Execution Completeness & Template Quality (25%)**: Working prototype, template fidelity, and practical real-world utility.
3. **Data Accuracy & Analytical Rigor (20%)**: Calculation formulas, evidence citations, and logical integrity.
4. **Documentation & Presentation Clarity (15%)**: Executive case study, clean typography, and 3-5 min Loom presentation.
5. **Tool Mastery & Automation Efficiency (15%)**: Efficient usage of domain-specific tech stacks and best practices.
